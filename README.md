# iguanodon.space

A music guessing game inspired by Hitster. Listen to songs and guess the song facts!

Live at: https://iguanodon.space

## How to Play

1. Choose a set: Standard, Rock, or Hip Hop
2. Click "Draw Card" to play a random song
3. Listen and guess the song facts
4. Click "Reveal" to see the answer
5. Click "Next Song" to continue

## Song Sets

- **Standard** (1000 songs): Pop, Rock, Soul, Electronic, Jazz, and more from 1950s-2020s
- **Rock** (1000 songs): Classic Rock, Metal, Punk, Grunge, and all rock subgenres
- **Hip Hop** (1000 songs): Old School to Modern Rap, from the 1980s golden era to today

## Bingo Mode

Each song displays with a random neon color glow (green, yellow, pink, blue, or purple) for use with Hitster Bingo cards.

## Requirements

- A web browser
- Spotify account (free or premium) to play the embedded tracks
